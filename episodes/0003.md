In Episode 3, Adam is on vacation, but Alex, Geoff and Stephen are here to wrap up our "meet the hosts" segment as we talk to Stephen about his tech journey to owning his own business.


We also dive into the sad march of MinIO, a once highly praised S3 object storage provider, towards abandoning open source and no longer being avaialble.  We also discuss the necessity of S3 storage in a home lab and alternatives to look into.


And finally, Geoff talks to us about his email journey over the years and how he recently discovered his favorite email provider added an API recently that has unlocked some useful functionality.  We also discuss email in general and uses for email forwarding services like SimpleLogin.


Topics: MSP, Minio, S3 object storage, Garage, SeaweedFS, MXroute, email, SimpleLogin, Snapraid, Jellyfin, GitHub, Trivy, FCC, routers
