## What we cover

* Why Stephen thinks the days of free email are over, and how paid accounts, separate admin credentials, and healthy suspicion can protect you and your family from AI-era phishing and phone scams.
* Adam's couch "battle station" powered by RustDesk, the concerns around the project's questionable open source history, and remote desktop alternatives like RDP, Sunshine and Moonlight, and MeshCentral.
* Geoff's love-hate relationship with the Snapmaker U1 and how it stacks up against Bambu Lab for beginners and tinkerers, plus the multi-tool H3C and Prusa's INDX.

First up, Stephen describes a convincing Google account recovery scam that starts with a call and tries to persuade the target to approve an OAuth prompt. The hosts discuss why relying on free email for banking and other critical accounts can leave people with little recourse after a takeover, and why paid email administration, separate admin accounts, strong authentication and helping family members recognize scams can reduce the damage. They also stress that email itself is not a secure protocol for sensitive information.

Next, Adam shares how RustDesk helps him use an OP Windows tablet as a remote console for his Mac and work tools, and how that can be complimented by Tailscale. The conversation weighs its responsiveness and self hosting options against concerns about project transparency and licensing, and considers MeshCentral, Sunshine and Moonlight, Steam streaming and RDP as alternatives.

Finally, Geoff gives his take on the Snapmaker U1 after the Bambu H3C announcement. The U1 prints well and offers local control and extensive firmware customization, but filament changes and multicolor workflows take more effort than Bambu’s integrated setup. The hosts compare the appeal of tinkering with the simplicity of a printer that works out of the box, and briefly discuss Prusa’s multi tool offering.
