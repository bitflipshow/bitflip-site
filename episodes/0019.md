## What we cover

* Stephen shares his first experience with the *Ocarina of Time* remake and the nostalgia surrounding classic console games.
* Netgate's pfSense Community Edition and Plus merger makes features free while moving major version upgrades behind a paid plan or Netgate hardware.
* Home Assistant's Connect Aux-2 sparks a conversation about PoE, audio, serial control, USB extension, and options for whole home audio.
* A Microsoft 365 outage and N-able N-central security incident lead to a broader discussion about zero days, urgent patching, and the strain on IT teams.


First up, Stephen talks about discovering the *Ocarina of Time* remake on Switch 2, despite having skipped the original as a kid. The game wins him over with its modern presentation and a few new surprises, prompting a detour into which consoles shaped different generations.

Next, the hosts unpack Netgate's plan to combine pfSense Community Edition and Plus. The combined release brings previously gated features to everyone, but free users will need to reinstall for future major version upgrades unless they subscribe or use Netgate hardware. Security and package updates remain available for online updates. The debate centers on whether charging for upgrade convenience is fair when remote, headless, or family deployments may turn a routine update into a hands on visit, and whether the change will push more users toward OPNsense.

After that, Nabu Casa has announced the Connect Aux-2, which gets us thinking about the Connect Aux 2's collection of ports and the smart home gear it could connect: PoE audio, RS 232 control, USB radios placed away from a server, and Bluetooth proxy support. They weigh the $69 price tag against simpler audio options such as Apollo Automation's CAST-1 and ESPhome based speakers from HouseWaves. The Aux-2 may be a niche purchase for this group, but it could be a useful bridge for people with older audio equipment or awkwardly placed radios.

Finally, the conversation turns to Microsoft's major 365 outage and Stephen's experience as an MSP operator during N-able N-central's shutdowns after multiple zero day vulnerabilities were disclosed. The hosts separate speculation from confirmed details, while considering how an exposed remote management controller could put an entire client fleet at risk. The incident also feeds a bigger question: AI may help uncover vulnerabilities, but the resulting wave of urgent patches creates real operational load and leaves little time to wait and see.


Topics: pfSense, Netgate, OPNsense, Home Assistant, Nabu Casa, Music Assistant, whole-home audio, Microsoft 365 outage, zero-day vulnerabilities, patching.
